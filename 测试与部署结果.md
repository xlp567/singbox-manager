本次仅操作明确授权的练手机。签名升级和旧版首次信任边界、低优先级跟进及外部缓存限制见公开摘要和中英文手册。

## English

See the [public summary](reports/PUBLIC_VALIDATION.md) for sanitized evidence. Raw infrastructure identifiers, credentials, logs and full backups remain private.

v3.5.6 passed 241 tests on each of macOS and Debian. The authorized practice host passed three rounds of nine-protocol transfers and 27 egress cases, custom SOCKS, Schema-generated Snell, and ten disposable-systemd acceptance stages.

After a full backup, the manager was updated and egress policy applied through a separate transaction. Both existing nodes passed three external HTTPS requests before and after and rejected loopback/localhost targets. Credentials, runtime files, original 3X-UI/Xray processes and protected files remained unchanged. Only policy application briefly restarted sing-box; manager installation did not.

v3.5.5 historically passed 208 local tests for transactional stop and publication privacy; it was not deployed at that stage. The public summary records remaining follow-ups, initial trust and external-cache limitations.
